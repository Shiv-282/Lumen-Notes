Lumen is a liquid-glass notebook for Android.

<b>Write anywhere</b>
Tap any spot on the page and type — text lives right where you put it, side by side with your drawings.

<b>Draw with smooth ink</b>
Quadratic-smoothed strokes in six colors plus a full custom picker (hue ring and light-to-dark slider). Adjustable brush width.

<b>Lasso anything</b>
Circle any part of your note to move, copy, cut, or delete it — strokes and text alike.

<b>Organize your way</b>
Folders, tags, pinning, full search, and a trash with restore — all backed by a local Room database. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking.

<b>Liquid glass design</b>
A translucent glass interface with real refraction and blur. Tool panels are born out of the dock with a liquid pinch-off animation. Dark mode, pure white, and pure black themes included.

<b>Yours, offline</b>
Everything is stored on your device. No internet permission required.
